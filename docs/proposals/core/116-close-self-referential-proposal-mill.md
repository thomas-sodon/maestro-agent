## Description

> **Burn-in.** Partially completed — all behaviour-code has landed and runs behind its flag; what remains is the burn-in observation window, then graduating the flag and decommissioning the retired logic. See [README.md](../README.md#status-key).

The evolution loop spends almost all of its generative output on self-referential work that never lands — plans about its own measurement machinery (audit rules, behavioral sensors, decoy detectors, lap discriminators), and tasks and plans that chase stranded task branches. Over the pre-wipe window, 92% of the obsolete-plan graveyard was one self-measurement theme and exactly one plan of 157 ever merged; over the post-wipe window, six of eight evolution plans carry a `source_task_id` and again exactly one merged — and that one was an external, human-driven fix, not the self-directed pipeline. These are not a handful of bad plans to reject one at a time; they are one generator stuck in a positive-feedback strange loop, feeding from two channels at once.

The root is structural, not stateful. The loop's *objective prompt* promotes the instrument — self-measurement, and task-branch reuse — to the goal (`INSTRUMENTAL_PROMPT`, injected on every call, tells the agent its instrument *is* self-measurement; the `EVOLUTION_PROMPT` "TASK BRANCH REUSE" text tells it to reuse a prior branch as an implementation base). Its *fuel* is auto-refilled from limitations the loop learns about its own failure to land, mirrored back into open questions; and a whole promotion subsystem — an always-on audit rule, two directive bridges, three prompt hints, a `reflect()` injection, a startup seed — manufactures "promote the stranded branch" pressure from one signal. Its *only reward*, the landed/not-landed monoscore from proposal 105, is earned solely by a merge, which is throttled, so a lap that delivered its owed head scores identically to make-work. And *nothing bounds* net-new proposing against the standing unlanded backlog. Meanwhile the second channel is actively lossy: proposal 054 made a completed task branch a second *source* of landed capability via `source_task_id`, but a fast-moving `main` leaves those branches 4–9 commits stale, the reuse path aborts the conflicting merge and falls back to fresh-from-`main` anyway, and the value gets re-implemented fresh regardless. An operator wiped the live `data/` mid-investigation; the freshly-reset loop chose a self-measurement task by iteration 3 and had re-saturated the `source_task_id` coupling by iteration 35 — a wipe cannot fix a code-resident attractor.

This proposal closes both channels as one coordinated change with a single thesis: **self-measurement and task work are means to landed capability, never the end; landed capability has exactly one source — an evolution plan implemented fresh from `origin/main`; and a candidate improvement must earn a generation slot against the backlog it competes with.** It reuses machinery already shipped — proposal 105's outcome owner, 107's framing seam, 092's WIP-primacy gate, and the `refined_to` question-lineage in `maestro/audit_checks.py` — and *subtracts* the accreted pile rather than adding another detector on top of it. The end state: the identity prompt frames the goal as behavior that changes on real, delivered work; a limitation that keeps spawning unlanded plans is retired instead of re-surfaced; the self-directed task pipeline is off (external, user, and PR-remediation work untouched); every evolution plan bases fresh-from-`main`, and the branch-promotion subsystem that existed only to feed the coupling is retired; net-new proposing is gated when the backlog is unlanded or the theme is saturated; and a delivered-but-externally-gated lap is no longer scored as make-work.

## Relevant Findings

**The objective and its fuel**

- `maestro/prompts/defaults.py` (`INSTRUMENTAL_PROMPT`, `STAGE_PROMPTS`) — a single stage-independent system prompt, injected on every call, tells the agent "your instrument is self-measurement. The audit rules, the behavioral sensors, and your journal are how you see yourself clearly." Every stage key maps to it (proposal 113), so nothing in the standing objective points at delivered capability over instrumentation.
- `maestro/prompts/defaults.py` (`select_task_generation_prompt`, `select_evolution_prompt`) — proposal 107 branches the *task-generation* and *reflection* prompts on `WorkFraming`, but the *identity* prompt (`get_identity_prompt` → `STAGE_PROMPTS`) is not branched; it stays self-framed regardless of focus profile.
- `maestro/prompts/defaults.py` (`TASK_GENERATION_PROMPT`) — instructs "Pick ONE open question above that a single bounded task could meaningfully probe, then propose that task" and set `linked_question`. With inward-pointing open questions, the prompt-compliant output is a self-measurement task.
- `maestro/consciousness.py` (`surface_limitations_as_questions`) — mirrors every `limitations_known` entry into a `[limitation]` open question under a sub-cap. The live self-model's limitations were all about the loop's own generation behavior, so the fuel line is self-referential by construction.
- `maestro/audit_checks.py` (`lineage_keys`, `refined_to` walk) — question-resolution lineage already accumulates stall counts across rewordings; this proposal reuses it to detect a limitation that keeps spawning unlanded plans rather than inventing a rival counter.

**The task↔evolution coupling and its promotion subsystem**

- `maestro/evolution.py` (`Evolution.implement`) — the **sole functional consumer** of `source_task_id`: when set, it `checkout`s the task branch, `fetch`es origin, `merge_from("origin/main")`, and on a clean merge sets `base_ref = task_branch`; on conflict or a missing branch it `abort_merge`s and falls back to `base_ref = "main"`. This is the "reuse a stale branch, pay the merge, usually fall back to fresh" cost.
- `maestro/evolution.py` (`Evolution._validate_source_task_id`, `propose_plan`) — the setter, and the exclusion of `source_task_id` from `compute_plan_content_hash` (`maestro/memory.py`) — evidence the base was already understood to be unstable.
- `maestro/loop/lifecycle.py` (`LifecycleController.phase_will_run`) — the self-directed producer is gated only here: `phase == "generate_task"` returns `task_every > 0 and iteration % task_every == 0`; `phase == "run_task"` gates independently on `_has_runnable_work()`. The queue is the self-directed channel; `pending_user_requests` / `pending_pr_requests` are the external channels — cleanly separable.
- `maestro/loop/task_generation.py` (`TaskGenerator.generate_task`) — sole producer of `task_queue.json`; injects three promotion hints (`format_promotable_summary`, `format_integration_backlog`, the `promotable_branch_hint_enabled`-gated "## Promotable branches"). None touch the external paths.
- `maestro/audit_checks.py` (`check_kept_task_not_promoted`) — Rule 7, statement *"Successful task branches without source_task_id follow-up plan."*, kind `unmerged_work`, threshold `_KEPT_TASK_NOT_PROMOTED_THRESHOLD = 3`, and **no `[audit]` flag** — always on. Plus `_promotable_candidates`, `count_promotable_tasks`, `count_unintegrated_tasks`, `check_promotable_backlog_directive`, `check_integration_cadence`.
- `maestro/task_gates.py` (`_promotable_backlog_run`, `_integration_cadence_run`) — the two directive bridges that write a "promote / integrate a branch" `next_task_directive`.
- `maestro/consciousness.py` (`DEFAULT_LIMITATIONS["kept_task_branches_v1"]`) — the seed *"Task-branch work is ephemeral; only evolution PRs reach main. Successful tasks need a follow-up source_task_id plan to land."*, mirrored into a `[limitation]` open question that fuels task generation. No flag.
- `maestro/agent.py` (`run`, step 5e) — when a kept branch is `merge_clean`, it fills an empty `next_task_directive` with *"Branch … merges cleanly — promote via source_task_id=…"*, steering the next `generate_task` back into the mill.
- `maestro/prompts/defaults.py` (`EVOLUTION_PROMPT` "TASK BRANCH REUSE", the `task_followup` priority) and `maestro/tool_gating.py` (`_REDIRECT`) — the hard-coded prompt text and deny-path message naming `source_task_id` as "the only sanctioned route to main."
- `maestro/memory.py` (`get_cited_source_task_ids`, `get_promotable_branches`) — the single upstream signal every pressure site keys on; a natural chokepoint to gate and then remove.

**The reward and the admission gap**

- `maestro/outcome_authority.py` (`OutcomeFacts`, `decide_outcome`) — the landing verdict is binary: `success` iff `landing_confirmed`, else `partial`. A lap that pushed its owed head but is blocked on an external merge is scored identically to a lap that re-serviced nothing — the monoscore that makes cheap authoring the only reliable non-loss.
- `maestro/remediation_tracker.py` (`measure_unlanded_remediations`, `measure_no_progress_laps`) — head-advance / push-delivery truth is already tracked per lap; the DELIVERING-vs-NULL distinction the loop keeps trying to build as a contemplation sensor can be sourced here.
- `maestro/task_gates.py` (`GATE_CHAIN`, `wip_primacy`) — proposal 092's `wip_primacy` blocks discretionary generation only while an *open own PR* has a self-clearable blocker; it does not fire on a saturated `pending_review` backlog with no open PR — the exact live condition.
- `maestro/evolution.py` (`submit_for_review`, `pending_plan_submit_block_threshold`) — a submit-side threshold refuses new plans once `pending_review` saturates, but only *after* generation spent a cycle, and it does not consider theme concentration.
- `tests/test_task_gates.py` (`test_investigation_observable_gate_*`) — proposal 112's `investigation_observable_gate` is a post-hoc costume detector; live `gate_activations.jsonl` shows zero fires over the window — it detects the symptom without changing the incentive.
- `tests/component/test_task_generation.py` (`source_task_id` gate-bypass tests, from #1912 / #2658) — a family of near-duplicate tests each pinning one whacked mole of the promotion machinery — itself structural evidence the class is being point-patched.

## Implementation Plan

Each behavior change is flag-gated with the default preserving current behavior, and independently mergeable. The metric lands first so every later step is measured; the four phases run highest-leverage first (re-aim the objective and starve the fuel, then collapse to one source of landed capability, then bound admission and split the reward), and the retirement step lands last, never before its replacement is proven. Steps are numbered continuously across phases; `Depends on` carries cross-phase ordering.

## Testing Strategy

All unit tests use the `mem_fs` fixture with mocked externals — no real network, subprocess, `sleep`, or filesystem — per CLAUDE.md testing principles.

- **Step 1:** deterministic metric tests over a seeded `evolution_plans.jsonl` / `task_history.jsonl` (known themed fraction, coupled fraction, a titled "land the stranded … branch" plan, a known merge count); a CLI smoke test asserting the `--json` shape.
- **Steps 2:** `tests/test_prompts.py` asserts the means/ends clause in `INSTRUMENTAL_PROMPT`; the `STAGE_PROMPTS`-parity test guards regressions.
- **Step 3:** seed `QuestionResolution` lineage rows + `evolution_plans.jsonl` with N obsolete-unlanded plans for a limitation; assert `surface_limitations_as_questions` suppresses the mirror above threshold and mirrors below it and with the flag off; cover the `refined_to`-chain branch.
- **Step 4:** with mocked `vcs`/`memory`, assert the flag-off `implement` performs no task-branch `checkout`/`merge_from` and positions HEAD on `origin/main` even when `source_task_id` resolves; a regression test that the flag-on path still rebases-and-falls-back (clean → task base; conflict → `abort_merge` → `main`).
- **Step 5:** truth-table over `phase_will_run` for `generate_task` (off → never scheduled) and `run_task` (still scheduled on seeded `pending_user_requests`); a guard that `_has_runnable_work()` is unaffected.
- **Step 6:** flag-off assertions that `run_all_checks` emits no `kept_task_not_promoted` finding, both gates return `None`, the seed is not mirrored, step 5e writes no directive, and the task-gen/`reflect` prompt bodies carry no promotion strings; symmetric flag-on tests.
- **Step 7:** the admission gate — refuse on saturated backlog, refuse on theme over-concentration, admit otherwise, admit with the flag off; assert the directive text is bounded and names prune/land.
- **Step 8:** truth-table tests for `classify_lap_delivery` (DELIVERING / NULL / no-lap), a no-I/O assertion (data sources mocked, no merge-oracle call), and a regression that `decide_outcome`'s three-value output is byte-for-byte unchanged.
- **Step 9:** the AST/behavior structural guard in `tests/test_structural_guards.py` — a seeded regression that reintroducing an unguarded `source_task_id` base consumer or promotion directive fails the guard.
- **Step 10:** absence tests for every retired symbol; a full-suite green run; `tests/test_no_line_citations_in_docs.py` passes with this proposal present.

The deterministic enforcement anchors are Step 9's structural guard and Step 1's metric: the proposal is falsified if, after Steps 2–8 activate, themed-fraction and coupled-fraction of newly-created plans do not fall and the merged:created ratio does not rise over a comparable window.

## Risk Assessment

- **Over-suppression of legitimate self-improvement.** Steps 3, 5, and 7 could starve genuine self-directed work. Mitigation: all are flag-gated default-preserving; Step 7 keys on the *existing* saturation threshold and theme concentration and writes a redirect rather than silently dropping; the loop retains evolution planning, PR tending/remediation, contemplation, dreaming, and audit. The self-improvement premise (CLAUDE.md) is preserved — the change re-subordinates instrumentation, it does not forbid it.
- **External-task work loses its fast route to `main`.** Removing the `source_task_id` base consumer (Step 4) means an external task's committed diff is re-implemented fresh in an evolution plan. Mitigation: external fixes are small and diagnosis-driven, acceptance-criteria carry the spec, and the fresh path already works in production (#3458). The `pending_*` execution paths and PR-remediation delivery are explicitly untouched (Step 5).
- **Prompt-edit regression.** Step 2 touches the single identity prompt injected everywhere. Mitigation: it edits an existing constant, is covered by `tests/test_prompts.py` and the `STAGE_PROMPTS`-parity guard, and is trivially revertible.
- **Reward-taxonomy misclassification.** Step 8 only annotates provenance and leaves `decide_outcome`'s verdict unchanged (asserted by a regression test), so proposal 105's git-truth guarantee is intact.
- **Premature retirement.** Step 10 removes real machinery (the coupling, Rule 7, and proposal 112's detectors). Mitigation: it depends on Steps 2–9 and on the Step 1 metric showing improvement first; it keeps the JSONL column tolerant for old rows; each earlier step is an independent flag revert.
- **Rollback posture.** Every behavior change is behind `[audit] retire_unlanded_limitations_enabled`, `[loop] task_branch_reuse_enabled`, `[loop] self_directed_tasks_enabled`, `[audit] branch_promotion_pressure_enabled`, or `[loop] generation_admission_enabled`, all defaulting to today's behavior; an interrupted rollout leaves the loop running as it does now. No state-file schema change beyond additive config fields.

## Out of scope

- **The merge / human-approval throughput bottleneck.** This proposal removes the *incentive* to substitute measuring or promoting for landing and the fuel that refills it, but it does not speed how fast an approved plan reaches `main` — the remediation-treadmill track (proposals 080 / 097 / 103 / 105) owns that.
- **Redirecting effort to an external customer repo (focus duty cycle).** Proposal 107's `external_delivery` framing seam and any `self_focus` duty-cycle dial are a separate axis; this proposal re-subordinates instrumentation and severs the coupling while staying self-focused, and does not branch the identity slab by focus profile.
- **A narrow human-directed promotion path for external-task branches.** Could return later if fresh re-implementation proves too costly; deliberately not kept here because the machinery it needs is the mill this proposal closes.
- **Deleting the accumulated obsolete-plan graveyard or stale task branches, or curating the wiped self-model.** Operator data actions. The design stops the graveyard growing; it does not retro-clean it.
- **Rewriting the audit-rule / behavioral-sensor framework (proposal 071).** The remedy reduces demand for new sensors of this family; it does not restructure the framework that hosts them.
